# Quality Checklist

Run this before final output.

## Universal checks

- Is the output mode clear?
- Is the story or purpose clear?
- Does each panel/shot serve a function?
- Are core subjects consistent?
- Are style, color, and camera language coherent?
- Are constraints specific enough to prevent drift?
- Did the response stop grilling once enough information existed?

## Storyboard image checks

- Is the aspect ratio appropriate, defaulting to 16:9 for cinematic storyboards?
- Is panel count reasonable, max 16?
- Are labels short and readable?
- Does the layout avoid overcrowding?
- Are reference strip and technical bar used only when useful?
- Does the prompt mention character/product consistency?
- Does it avoid long text inside the image?

## Video prompt checks

- Does duration fit shot count?
- Is shot density appropriate for the genre?
- Does every major result have a visible cause when needed?
- Do actions continue logically across shots?
- Is audio included only where useful?
- Are subtitles/screen text omitted unless explicitly requested?
- Is the prompt feasible for current AI video generation?

## Auto-simplification rules

When overloaded:

- reduce characters to 1–2 core subjects;
- reduce scene changes;
- merge redundant shots;
- make actions more readable;
- split into multiple clips;
- remove unnecessary text overlays;
- use a stronger continuity constraint;
- move explanation out of image text into prompt text.
