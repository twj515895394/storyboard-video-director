# Negative Constraint Snippets

Use adaptively; do not paste all of them blindly.

## Storyboard layout

- Avoid crowded, unreadable layout.
- Avoid dense paragraphs inside panels.
- Avoid wrong panel order or duplicated panels.
- Avoid cheap stock-template design.

## Subject consistency

- Do not redesign the core character/product/robot.
- Maintain consistent face, outfit, scale, color, and key props.
- Do not swap reference asset purposes.

## Video continuity

- No unexplained teleporting.
- No object state reset between shots.
- No result without visible cause.
- No sudden environment change unless it is an intentional transition.

## Realistic/cinematic style

- No low-quality rendering.
- No plastic skin.
- No excessive polish unless commercial style is requested.
- No magical effects unless explicitly part of the concept.

## Text/subtitles

- Do not generate subtitles or screen text unless explicitly requested.
- Keep any storyboard labels short, clean, and legible.
